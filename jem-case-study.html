JEM — Jewellery Expense Manager

A mobile-first expense, approval and payment app for a jewellery business. One installable app for desktop and phone, with a 5-role approval chain, built-in controls against duplicate and backdated claims, and reports that finance can export.

The problem

Expenses, cash counts and approvals were spread across separate apps and spreadsheets. Month-end reconciliation was slow, and nothing stopped the same bill from being claimed twice, an old bill from being back-dated, or a manager from approving their own claim.

The solution

A single app where each person sees only what their role needs: staff submit claims, managers approve, accounts pay and reconcile, and the MD reviews reports. The rules live in the app itself rather than in people's memory.

  • Approval chain with guards: five roles, and self-approval is blocked.
  • Duplicate-bill detection: flags the same bill before it reaches an approver.
  • Submission rules: date window, receipt required above a set amount, amount limit, two decimal places, description required.
  • Cash reconciliation: book closing compared with the counted cash, with matched / short / excess / missing / pending status.
  • Analyst-style reports: KPIs, tables and takeaways, with CSV and PDF export and hover / tap chart tooltips.

Key design decisions

Single-file PWA

Plain JavaScript and CSS variables, with a service worker and web manifest. It loads fast, installs from the browser and keeps working offline.

Safe writes

Every save carries a request ID, so a retry after a poor network never creates a duplicate record. Server time (IST) decides dates, not the phone clock.

Spreadsheet protection

Text beginning with =, +, - or @ is neutralised before it is written to a sheet, which blocks formula injection from user input.

Backend by design

OTP login, role checks, write locking and an audit log on Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. The public demo runs on sample data in the browser only.

Outcome

A working and tested demo that covers the full flow from claim to payment to reconciliation, with the rules enforced consistently on both the app side and the backend side. Recruiters and reviewers can try every role in under a minute, with no setup and no sign-up.
